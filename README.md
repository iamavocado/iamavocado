## 👨‍💻 About Me
I am a biologist and software developer based in Panama. I combine my scientific background with software engineering to create efficient, structured, and scalable technological solutions.

I am focused on developing my skills in:
- Full-Stack Web and Mobile Applications
- Business Management Solutions
- Scientific Research and Data Analysis

## 🚀 Background
My journey is a blend of science and technology. My background in biology has given me a strong foundation in research, deep statistical analysis, and methodical approaches to complex problem-solving. 

Alongside my scientific pursuits, I have channeled my passion for technology into software development. I have learned to build robust applications from scratch, manage databases, and optimize architectures on both the client and server sides.

## 🎯 Current Focus
- **Software Engineering:** Architecting and developing cross-platform mobile applications and responsive web pages. 
- **Scientific Research:** Working on research focused on bioremediation and bioinformatics.

## 📌 Experience & Projects
### 💻 Mobile Applications & Web Development
Designing and building versatile mobile applications and responsive websites. 
- **Mobile Solutions:** Developing robust cross-platform mobile apps. Experience includes architecting scalable mobile applications for operational management, ensuring reliable local data synchronization and high performance in offline scenarios.
- **Web Development:** Creating functional, responsive web pages and user-friendly web interfaces.

### 🔬 Bioremediation Research
Applied study on treating industrial effluents utilizing the degradative capacity of species from the order Polyporales.
- **Focus:** Fungal taxonomy, viability testing, and statistical analysis of results.
